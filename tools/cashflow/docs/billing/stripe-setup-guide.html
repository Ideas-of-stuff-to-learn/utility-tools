Stripe Setup

Getting your Stripe account ready

Follow these steps in order. You only need to do this once.

About 10–15 minutes
1
Create your Stripe account
Go to stripe.com and sign up. Use your real name and business details.
  • →Go to stripe.com and click "Start now"
  • →Enter your email address and create a password
  • →Verify your email when the confirmation arrives
  • →Fill in the business questions — choose Individual / Sole trader if unsure
  • →You can skip the bank account step for now — we'll come back to that later
2
Make sure Test Mode is on
Test mode lets you try everything out without real money moving. It should be on by default.
dashboard.stripe.com

⚠️
Look for the orange "Test mode" badge in the top bar. If you don't see it, there's a toggle in the top-right corner of the dashboard — make sure it's on.
3
Get your API keys
These are the codes that connect your website to Stripe. You need two of them.
  • 1Click Developers in the top-right corner of the dashboard
  • 2Click API keys in the left menu that appears
  • 3You'll see the two keys shown below

4
Add a webhook endpoint
This tells Stripe where to send notifications when payments happen.
  • 1Still in Developers, click Webhooks in the left menu
  • 2Click "+ Add endpoint"
  • 3Your developer will give you the exact URL to enter here
  • 4After saving, click the endpoint and click "Reveal" next to Signing secret
